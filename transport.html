What to ride & how

Practical instructions by transport type.

🚆

Airport Rail Link (ARL)

Best first connection from your hotel

Very useful

How you use it

  • Walk from PANNARAI Hotel to Ratchaprarop (A7).
  • For BTS: Ratchaprarop → Phaya Thai (A8), then transfer to BTS.
  • For MRT: Ratchaprarop → Makkasan (A6), then walk to Phetchaburi MRT.
  • Service is generally 05:30–00:00; published fares are ฿15–45 (~₱28–₱84).
  • No advance booking is normally needed; buy/use the fare system at the station.
Hotel → Ratchaprarop ARL (walking)
🚈

BTS Skytrain

Best for Siam, Sukhumvit and Saphan Taksin

Easy for tourists

How you use it

  • Connect via ARL Ratchaprarop → Phaya Thai.
  • For Siam: transfer at Phaya Thai to BTS Sukhumvit Line and ride toward Siam.
  • For Asiatique by public transport: continue to Siam, change to Silom Line, then ride to Saphan Taksin.
  • Buy a Single Journey Card or Rabbit Card. A new Rabbit Card has a ฿120 (~₱224) issuance fee before stored value.
  • BTS ticket-office hours are roughly 06:00–00:00. No seat reservation/online booking is needed.
Example: hotel → Siam using public transport
🚇

MRT Metro

Best for Mega Plaza / Old Town connections

Card tap available

How you use it

  • Hotel → Ratchaprarop ARL → Makkasan → walk to Phetchaburi MRT.
  • For Mega Plaza: Blue Line to Sam Yot, then walk.
  • For Wat Pho/Grand Palace area, Sanam Chai is a useful MRT stop.
  • Blue/Purple Line gates officially support qualifying international contactless Visa/Mastercard; use the same card to enter and exit.
  • The official BEM MRT app shows fares, travel time, exits and last trains.
Example: hotel → Sam Yot (Mega Plaza/Old Town)
🚌

Bangkok buses

Cheapest, but less beginner-friendly

Use ViaBus

How you use it

  • Use ViaBus for nearby stops, bus numbers and real-time locations where supported.
  • General government guidance lists ordinary/air-con buses around ฿8–25 (~₱15–₱47); actual route/operator fares can vary.
  • Have small cash ready unless the specific bus clearly supports another method.
  • For six travelers, rail or Grab/Bolt may be simpler on short central-Bangkok trips.
Example: hotel → centralWorld; use ViaBus to choose the bus
🚤

Khlong Saen Saep canal boat

Fast local shortcut around Pratunam

Local experience

How you use it

  • Walk to Pratunam Pier, the central interchange between the Golden Mount and NIDA lines.
  • Westbound stops include Sapan Hua Chang (Siam/MBK) and Panfa Leelard (Golden Mount/Old Town side).
  • Current 2026 fares are about ฿16–26 (~₱30–₱48) by distance.
  • Pay the conductor on board and keep your paper ticket if transferring.
  • ViaBus can help with some boat/transit tracking.
Hotel → Pratunam Pier (walking)
⛴️

Chao Phraya river boats

Scenic riverside option

Tourist-friendly

How you use it

  • The easiest connection is BTS to Saphan Taksin, then walk to Sathorn Pier.
  • Effective Oct. 6, 2026: Orange ฿19 (~₱35), Yellow ฿24 (~₱45), Red ฿33 (~₱61), depending on service/day.
  • Asiatique’s official free shuttle operates 16:00–23:30, about every 25–30 min, from Sathorn.
  • Your Day 9 Asiatique transfer is already booked, so this is mainly backup/reference.
Example: hotel → Sathorn Pier
🚕

Grab / Bolt / taxi

Best with shopping bags or late hours

Door-to-door

How you use it

  • Grab: set pickup/drop-off and choose JustGrab/GrabCar/other available vehicle.
  • Bolt: set destination and choose an available category; Bangkok may offer XL depending on availability.
  • Both show fare estimates/upfront pricing; tolls/surcharges may be separate.
  • Grab advance booking is generally 75 minutes to 90 days ahead; Bolt advertises scheduling up to 90 days.
  • For six people, compare one large vehicle versus two normal cars.
Driving example: hotel → Mega Plaza

Apps to install before leaving the Philippines

Yes—several can be installed in the Philippines and used when you arrive in Thailand.

🚕 Grab

English ✅PH download ✅Thailand ✅Advance booking ✅

Yes: the same Grab app/account can be used in supported Southeast Asian countries including the Philippines and Thailand. Grab says a local SIM is not required, but you still need mobile data/Wi‑Fi and account access.

Grab accepts cash/cards; Thailand currently notes a foreign-payment fee for foreign cards/wallets, so check the final fare screen.

iPhone PH ↗Android ↗Thailand info ↗

🚕 Bolt

English ✅PH App Store ✅Bangkok ✅Schedule rides ✅

The passenger app is available in the Philippine App Store and Bolt operates in Thailand. The same global app shows ride types based on your current city.

Keep internet access and your account verification method available; payment methods and vehicle categories vary by location.

iPhone PH ↗Android ↗Thailand info ↗

🚌 ViaBus

English ✅PH App Store ✅Bus tracking ✅Boats/transit ✅

Useful for Bangkok buses and some boat/transit tracking. The iPhone app is listed in the Philippine App Store and supports English.

Install it before the trip; in Bangkok enable location to find stops, routes and ETAs where available.
